## 2. Decisions (fixed for M5.5; each with its reason)

D1. **Heat is fed by what the police see, and the police are always there.**
`POLICE.budget[0]` becomes 2; a crime in a unit's sight pays double heat
and puts the pursuit on `detected` the same step. Reason: DESIGN §13.3; a
city with no police at heat 0 cannot answer a crime, and a ratchet only
rare events move never reads as a ratchet.

D2. **A coin exists only at a goal or on the current job's route.** The
static layout keeps the gate lines (shortened), the ramp arcs, the ring
approaches and the day's thirty caches; the trails, fillers and sweeps are
deleted, not tuned down. Reason: DESIGN §13.5, Marcin's verdict twice; the
research's rule (breadcrumbs trace an action, a carpet inflates to nothing).

D3. **Route coins are laid whole at job start through the run-time pool.**
Not streamed ahead: the pool holds 512, a route needs at most 120, and a
whole line is what the radar and the arrow can show. Reason: the intro's
mechanism exists (`Coins.addExtra` / `clearExtra`) and the cost of a
streamed variant is a second system for no visible gain.

D4. **The goal line is the job line.** One DOM element (`ui/jobs.ts`) shows
the running job or, idle, the chain step or the default (`TAKE A JOB`,
`BANK IT`, `LOSE THEM`). Reason: one line, one marker (DESIGN §13.4); two
elements would fight for the same 24 px.

D5. **The chain lives in the save as one byte and pays nothing.** Reason:
a reward would make it a job list; the reward is the next step, and a byte
survives every migration.

D6. **Driver personality is two bytes on the traffic record, seeded at
spawn from the seed and the slot.** Reason: deterministic (the tour pins
and the balance script must not change with the wall clock), no allocation,
and the planner reads two arrays it already indexes.

D7. **The police driving mode is a flag on the record, read by the same
planner.** No second follower: `plan` and `driveBody` branch on
`policeMode[i]` at the junction claim, the leader gap and the curve
handles. Reason: record 26 (police are a planner over traffic agents) is
what made M4 affordable; the mode changes three rules, not the system.

D8. **Catch-up only out of view.** Within the player's view cone a unit's
desired speed is the player's plus `pressure` m/s, never the catch-up.
Reason: NFS Heat's visible rubber band is what players call cheating; GTA
refills and catches up out of sight.

D9. **The caches and the arrivals in view use the date seed and the police
seed already in `Dailies` and `cover.ts`.** Reason: record 48; nothing new
is placed, a fixed spot list is ordered by the day.

D10. **The taxi is a paint and a roof box on the compact; the bus is a
stretched heavy and ships only if its profile costs under a day.** Reason:
DESIGN §13.8; the taxi is variety for the price of a paint, the bus is a
new collider, a new profile and a swap case.

D11. **The wall scales by CSS only** (`clamp` on the width, a root type
size from the viewport height). Reason: the ten-size screens already
exist; one rule, ten checks.

### 3.2 Step order in `SimWorld.step()`

Unchanged from M5 §3.2, with two insertions: `Police.step` publishes
`crimeSeen()` before `Heat.step` reads the ring (Heat asks the pursuit
whether the crime's tick was seen), and `Jobs.step` lays or clears the
route line before `Coins.step` picks (a coin laid this step is pickable
this step, so the cap at the target is never missed on arrival).

## 8. API facts, traps and the research

- `Heat` reads the ring after `Police.step` (§3.2); `crimeSeen()` exists on
  `Police` and reads the last sight tick per unit and per parked patrol, so
  a crime is "seen" at 6-step sampling resolution; that is the rule, not a
  bug.
- `Traffic.plan` runs per agent per step for 48 records: the overtake scan
  must use the lane index (`laneIndex`, `MAX_ON_LANE`), never a pool scan.
- `lanes.connectionLength` and the curve builder take the handle ratio from
  a constant today (`HANDLE_RATIO`); the mode's 0.25 is a second table, not
  a parameter on the hot path.
- `Coins.addExtra` / `clearExtra` exist for the intro's line and take
  `CoinPoint[]`; `EXTRA_COINS_MAX` 512; the view registers on `extraSerial`.
- The save is v1 (`sim/save/format.ts`, the migrations table); v2 adds
  `chain`, `borrowHints`, `caches` (date + a 30-bit set) with sanitising per
  field; reserve the slice-6 field (`hot`) in v2 so the format moves once.
- `?date=` drives the dailies and the police seed already (`Dailies.setDate`
  at boot and once a minute); the caches take the same string; test
  sessions without it get the first thirty spots.
- The radar draws job rings and the doors already (`ui/minimap.ts`,
  `JOB_COLORS`); blips and the disc are two more layers in the same
  painter, maths in `minimapModel.ts` with Node pins.
- The ticker and the goal line share the top centre with the intro's
  captions and the key hints: precedence captions > ticker > goal line >
  hints, one visible at a time.
- The balance script excludes itself from other runs (`vite.config.ts`);
  its tables are the record for slices 0, 1 and 4.
- Perf noise on the MX330 is ±3 fps: alternate builds before calling a
  regression; nothing else renders on the machine during a measured run.

## 9. Five-minute playtest script (for the gate report)

1. A fresh profile: the intro, the door, then the goal line: does `TAKE A
   JOB · 320 m` tell you what to do without the sentence on the wall.
2. Drive dirty for a minute: rams, a billboard, past a patrol at speed. Do
   the stars fill, do the `+n` pops read, do the patrols turn on you, and
   does level 2 arrive inside five minutes.
3. A delivery: are the coins on your road and only there, can you take
   them all, does `48/48 · TIP` land; between jobs, is the road empty.
4. Level 2 chase: do the cops drive like cops (around traffic, through the
   junctions), does traffic pull over, does the search disc say where not
   to be, does the ring say when you are free.
5. The highway: is there someone to overtake and someone overtaking you.
6. The garage: does it read as a place; the wall at your screen size.
7. Report what felt wrong before what worked.
