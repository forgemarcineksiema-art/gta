# M5.5 gate report: the whole game before the platform

Date: 2026-09-23. Contract: `docs/M5.5_PLAN.md` (slices 0–21, §7 gate
criteria). Session log: `docs/PROGRESS.md` (an entry per slice with what it
built and its pins, and the gate's entries with the numbers below).

## What was built

Everything discussed after Marcin's M5 playtest, before any platform work:
the polish and the backlog's play items, update 1 ("the air"), update 2
(the activities) and the city's own models.

- **Heat that moves** (slice 0): two patrols on the beat at heat 0; a crime
  a unit sees counts double and starts the chase; reckless driving,
  speeding past a patrol and the chase itself raise the heat; fault follows
  speed. The `+n` pops, the level sting and the ticker line.
- **Coins as breadcrumbs** (slice 1): coins only at billboards, ramps, a
  job's route (a 10 % tip for taking them all) and thirty daily caches.
- **The goal line and the chain** (slice 2): one line always names the next
  thing to do and the arrow points at it; the first quarter hour is a
  six-step chain with a card per step.
- **Traffic with character** (slice 3): a driver per car (pace, time gap,
  one in eight bad), overtakes on the highway, the flinch, the pull-over for
  a lit unit, the pass round a dead car, the angry driver, the clone cap,
  thinner streets at heat 4–5.
- **The police as a different animal** (slice 4): pressure instead of a
  flat speed, the police driving mode, the refill by level, arrivals in
  view, units and the search disc on the radar, the cooldown ring, the
  radio in the ticker, the siren by level, a bounty for every 10 s of chase.
- **The garage dressed, the wall sized** (slice 5) and **bring it home, pay
  to keep it** (slice 6): props along the walls; the wall scales at 1536
  and 1920 wide; a car driven home is kept for 30 % of its price.
- **Update 1, the air** (slices 7–9): a covered street per district with the
  camera's occlusion rule; the highway over its four crossings on 7.5 m
  decks; the helicopter from level 4, its searchlight its sight, the cover
  its only answer.
- **Update 2, the activities** (slices 10–15): four time trials on a coin
  line (medals), four street races against three rubber-banded rivals,
  takedown rage and mayhem zones, taxi fares with hot passengers, the skill
  chain (near misses, the oncoming lane, drifts, flights, up to ×5), the
  jump and billboard hunts, the full-screen map on Tab.
- **Extras** (slices 16–18): the ice-cream truck as a hidden car, the giant
  ball, the crumple; traffic lights at the nine downtown crossings, parked
  cars in the bays, the takedown's side cut; the officer with the ticket
  book, eight pursuit breakers, the donut shop, the news lines.
- **Life's models** (slices 19–20): eight civilian bodies with their own
  footprint, collider, mass and habits (buses on the avenues, taxis round
  the tower, trucks in the Works); four pedestrian silhouettes with a walk,
  dressed by district.
- **The gate** (slice 21): ten long pins fixed in the game (billboards past
  the ramps, a gentler arrest, a wider pull-over, the standoff, the cold
  open capped at level 2, lanes by height); the traffic's two pair scans
  made cheap (below); the balance script's measurement made robust; the
  docs.

The first gate runs were a regression by the protocol (a frame p95 above
the bases in both runs): the traffic phase had doubled. The profile named
two pair scans over the pool, which the parked cars grew from 48 to 58
records: `aheadGap` called the police and parking predicates for every
pair before the cheap geometry, and `unstick` took a square root and a
spacing for every pair, four passes a step. Both now reject by position
first; the logic is unchanged (the same pose after a minute at two seeds)
and the traffic step in Node fell 290–360 → 176–196 µs. After it: frame
p95 back to the bases' 33.3, the fps 2–3 under them (the MX330's noise band
is ±3), the step p95 under budget.

## How to run

- `npm start`, then `http://localhost:4173/`: the production build, the
  stamp `0.5.5+<commit>` on the pause screen. A first visit (or `?fresh=1`)
  plays the cold open once.
- New test parameters: `?police=off` (no police, for the order e2e). M5's
  and M4's still work (`?fresh=1`, `?date=`, `?job=`, `?bot=job`, `?heat=`,
  `?spawn=`, `?ad=off`, `?dev=1`); `docs/DEV.md` lists them.
- `npm run verify`, `verify:gate`, `game`, `heat`, `city`, `life`,
  `screens`, `perf`, `balance`.

## Five-minute playtest script

1. `?fresh=1`: the cold open, the door, then the goal line: does `TAKE A
   JOB · 320 m` tell you what to do without the sentence on the wall.
2. Drive dirty for a minute: rams, a billboard, past a patrol at speed. Do
   the stars fill and the `+n` pops read, do the patrols turn on you. Stop
   at a downtown red light with a unit behind: the officer walks up with
   the ticket book; floor it before the pad fills.
3. A delivery: are the coins on your road and only there, does the tip
   land; between jobs, is the road empty.
4. A chase at level 2–3: do the cops drive like cops and does traffic pull
   over; hold Tab for the map; drive through a scaffold tower with a unit
   behind you. At level 4, the helicopter: hide under a covered street or
   an overpass.
5. One new ring: a gold trial, a lime race, a red or white zone; in a taxi,
   stop for a hailing walker.
6. Near misses, the oncoming lane, a drift: the chain top left; a wall
   loses it.
7. Palm Gardens' south edge: the ice-cream truck (listen for the tune);
   the ball south of the Works street; the donut shop.
8. The first hour: note the minute you buy the first car (the brief says
   5–7, the model says 3.9) and whether each purchase after it felt earned.
   Report what felt wrong before what worked.

## Tuning knobs and where they live

The tuning objects are live in the dev panel (backtick) unless noted.

- `src/sim/balance.ts` (`BALANCE`): `heat` (the crime points, the seen
  factor, speeding, the chase drip), `bag` (every event's pay, the chase
  bounty `pursuitPer10s`, the escape `escapePerLevel`), `coin` (values, the
  route tip, the caches), `skill` (every trick's points, the multiplier,
  the window), `hunts`, `stash`, `jobs.trial`, `jobs.race`, `jobs.zone`,
  `fares`, `prices`, `tierPrices`, `coldOpen.heatCap`.
- `src/sim/traffic/tuning.ts` (`TRAFFIC`): the drivers (paces, time gaps,
  the bad share), `overtake`, `flinch`, `pullOver`, `standoff`, `gawk`,
  `angry`, `densityByLevel`, `signals` (green, amber, all red, offset),
  `parked` (share, band, cap).
- `src/sim/police/tuning.ts` (`POLICE`): `budget` by level, `pressure`, the
  refill cadence, the arrivals, `heli`, `arrest` (`near`, `nearSpeed`),
  `busted`.
- Code constants, not in the panel: `BREAKER` and `BREAKERS`
  (`sim/city/breakers.ts`), `DONUT_SHOP` (`sim/police/Donuts.ts`),
  `GIANT_BALL` and `STASH_SPOTS` (`sim/city/stash.ts`), `SIGNAL`
  (`sim/city/signals.ts`).

## Known issues

- **The balance script is red on (b) and (c).** The novice's income a
  minute is on DESIGN §3.3's target (2.9k against 2.6k), but the model's
  novice run lasts 2.4 minutes: the road bot waits in queues at the
  downtown lights with units behind it and is busted even at level 1, once
  in three minutes at every seed. So the first car comes at minute 3.9
  (the brief: 5–7) and one gap is 2.4 minutes (the floor: 3). The prices
  are left at the design's values rather than fitted to a bot's trap.
  Tried and reverted: the careful bot's overtake while chased, which made
  the novice a rammer (bag 4,079 a minute). Marcin's first hour decides;
  the knobs are `prices.compact` and tier 1 (over 13.6k keeps every gap
  over three minutes at these runs).
- **Level 4 catches the novice bot less than levels 2 and 3** at all three
  seeds (0.22 against 0.78 and 0.44 a minute): the streets thin to 60 %, so
  fewer queues trap it. A player's level 4 decides whether the heavies and
  the helicopter need more bite.
- **Perf is 2–3 fps under M5.1** at 4× CPU after the fix (53.7–53.9 against
  55.7–56.9, frame p95 equal): the traffic step is still 2.4 ms against 1.6
  (signals, parked cars, drivers, bodies), draws 133 against 99–102. Time
  to control 3.8–4.3 s against 3.3–3.4. All inside the budgets.
- **The quick `verify` runs 98–100 s of tests** (CLAUDE.md: under a
  minute); the pins over ~5 s belong in the long set (BACKLOG).
- **The bots' limits**, not the game's: race #23 at seed 42 fails from the
  test bot's reset loop at the city spawn; the naive hunter reaches 4 of 6
  wanted cars in 240 s (#10 and #16 not); the careful delivery bot takes
  43–54 of 54–62 route coins.
- **Not done inside the slices**: the garage keeps classes (a pickup driven
  home offers the van, slice 19); SELL / KEEP at a fence (slice 6); tighter
  junction curves for units (slice 4, cosmetic). All three in BACKLOG.
- **No music bed**: still needs Marcin's yes on the file, its source and its
  licence.
- **Pins changed beyond the three §5.1 names**, each with its reason in
  PROGRESS (the slice entries and "M5.5 gate: the long pins"): pins whose
  premise the milestone changed on purpose (the roster with the helicopter's
  place, the thinned streets, the chase plan's heading, the height above
  the overpasses, the bodies and looks lists scoped to what spawns, the
  delivery pin's careful bot). None was loosened to pass a defect.
