## 1. The design — decided (Marcin), 2026-09-25

Marcin asked for the map drawn from above and a word on the world. The
atlas (the generator's own chunks, every building, road and prop, drawn
top-down) showed what the talk then named: a simple generator with a few
authored roads. Seven streets on each side run past the ring road to the
sea wall and end there; a square city of 36 blocks, each the same, with
nothing to do in them; a ring of parks round it all, the grid's leftover;
flat ground, one collision plane since M2; and three names the places do
not keep. There is no palm in Palm Gardens (the game's only palms stand on
the Quay); Coral Quay is the grid with a promenade on two edges and two
small piers; Crown Heights has no hill. He took a hand-drawn plan whole
(the two sketches in `docs/island/`), asked what the brief's four games
have that it lacks, took the answer, and asked to write it down and start.
It sits before the platform: the map is what a player plays. The design
and the contract are this one file, as he wants a design talk kept
(`docs/DESIGN.md` §21 points here).

### 1.1 The decision

- **The plan is drawn by hand**: the coast, the ground's heights, the
  districts' areas, the main roads (the highway, the avenues, the set
  pieces' roads) and where every placed thing stands. What fills it still
  comes from the seed: each district's own streets by its rule, the lots,
  the buildings, the things on the pavements. This changes the brief's "the
  city is generated procedurally from a seed" to "the plan by hand, the
  rest from the seed" (Marcin, 2026-09-25).
- **The size stays**: 2.54 km² of land (1.8 × 1.6 km, the bay and the port's
  basin cutting in; the grid island is 2.48), and 0.09 km² more for the
  airfield's causeway and the islet. At 150 km/h the island is crossed in
  about 45 s and the highway's lap (5.4 km, the grid's ring's length) takes
  about 2 min, long enough for a chase; the fog shows about half a
  kilometre, so the island is three or four views across; what failed was
  sameness, not size. The runway needs 670 m and the hill about 500 m to
  rise 50 m; a smaller island loses them.
- **The names stay and come true**: the hill under Crown Heights, the palms
  and the gardens in Palm Gardens, the quay and the coral in Coral Quay.
- **No street ends in a field**: every street ends on another; the only
  ends are a set piece's own (the lighthouse's loop, the pier's end over
  the water).

### 1.2 The island (sketch 1, `docs/island/plan.jpg`)

The sketches are drawn north up and east right; the world's +X is west, so
`plan.ts` turns them by half a turn (§7).

- **The ground**: a hill under Crown Heights, 52 m at the summit, falling
  to cliffs on the north-west; gentle hills in Palm Gardens (up to 18 m);
  low ground (2–6 m) by the port and the bay. A height field; the roads are
  cut and filled into it.
- **The coast**: cliffs (Crown), quays (the port and its basin), a bay with
  a marina (the Quay), a beach along the whole south, golf and dunes on the
  south-west headland, a low rocky west. No ring of parks.
- **The highway**: a loop along the coast, two lanes each way, with a
  tunnel under the hill, a viaduct over the port's basin and a bridge over
  the bay's mouth.
- **The centre**: a roundabout with six avenues. Crown Avenue is one
  straight line from the bay up to the summit, so the tower is seen from
  the water and the bay from the top; the harbour road, the east and west
  avenues, Palm Avenue to the beach.
- **Crown Heights**: a grid over the hill, 90 m blocks, steep streets with
  flat crossings (a car flies at each crossing); the summit's plaza round
  the Crown Tower; the serpentine down the west side; the quarry; the
  multi-storey car park with a roof-to-roof jump; the police headquarters
  by the roundabout; the hideout.
- **Sunset Works**: the port (the basin, cranes, a container maze); the dry
  canal a car drives in, with bridges over it; the railway and a freight
  train at the crossings; the Waterworks; the scrapyard; the airfield on
  its causeway, a 670 m runway with the mega-ramp at its end and the islet
  200 m beyond.
- **Palm Gardens**: Palm Avenue lined with palms; the botanic garden with
  the Glasshouse and its paths; crescents that follow the ground; the golf
  course and the dunes; the beach; shortcuts through back gardens (fences).
- **Coral Quay**: the bay with a marina (a jump between piers), a pleasure
  pier with a ferris wheel, a reef in the shallows, the lighthouse on its
  spit, the Coral Hotel on the water, the stadium with an oval, and a giant
  rubber duck afloat in the bay.

### 1.7 The rules this plan keeps — set here

- **R1. The plan by hand, the fill from the seed.** `src/sim/island/plan.ts`
  holds the coast, the heights, the districts, the main roads, the set
  pieces and every placed thing; the districts' own streets, the lots, the
  buildings and the props come from the seed by each district's rule.
- **R2. The names come true.** Palms and gardens in Palm Gardens, the hill
  under Crown Heights, the quay and the reef in Coral Quay; pinned.
- **R3. No street ends in a field.** Every road's end is a junction or a
  set piece's own loop; pinned on the graph.
- **R4. One ground.** A height field per chunk; every "on the ground"
  (spawns, resets, traffic, pedestrians, props, coins, rings, signs,
  markers, the camera's floor) reads `groundHeight(x, z)`.
- **R5. Lanes stay lanes.** The network is the same `RoadGraph` (polyline
  lanes with a height); the traffic, the police, the bot, the way and the
  radar's route keep their code.
- **R6. The grid until the switch.** Nothing the grid game reads changes
  before slice 18; the island is `?map=island`.
- **R7. The budgets hold** (§4.4): the terrain is coarse where it is flat;
  a chunk's ground, roads and buildings stay one merged mesh.

### Phase B — the ground

#### Slice 2 — the height field (1.5 days)

`src/sim/island/ground.ts`: `groundHeight(x, z)` from the plan: the
natural height, the main roads graded into it (a road's profile smoothed
along its length to its class's grade, 8 % for the highway and the
avenues, 16 % for Crown's streets; the ground within its half width and
2 m of shoulder set to the profile and blended to the natural height over
12 m), the canal's channel, the quarry's pit, the beach's slope under the
water, the cliffs; `Island` (`src/sim/island/Island.ts`), the grid's `City`
for the island with what driving needs: a height field per 225 m chunk at
2 m (`RAPIER.ColliderDesc.heightfield`, terrain group, built when the chunk
enters the physics ring and freed when it leaves), the sea's boundary at
the coast, the spawns. `SimWorld` builds it for `map: 'island'`
(`?map=island` in `src/app/`); everything that needs the grid's `City`
reads null and stays off on the island until its slice (the jobs, the
coins, the props, the traffic).

Pins (`tests/sim/island/ground.test.ts`): 2.1 the height field matches
`groundHeight` within 1 cm at 200 random points; 2.2 a muscle car
handbraked across a 12 % slope moves less than 0.5 m in 5 s; 2.3 at 100
km/h over the hill's crest the car lands, settles within 1 s and stays
upright; 2.4 a 30 s drive across four chunk borders never loses more than
one step of a wheel's contact on the road; 2.5 a chunk's height field is
built in under 5 ms in Node.

As built: chunks of 250 m (10 × 8), the physics' ring 3 × 3 round the car,
a chunk's heights (about 20 ms of work) worked out ahead four columns a
step over the 5 × 5 round it and kept; a height field then builds in
0.1 ms, a step's share of the heights takes 0.4 ms (1.6 at the 99th
percentile), alone. Pin 2.5 counts the work (no chunk late, at most one
height field and four columns a step) and times only the build (the
median of 30): a step's wall clock under the suite's four workers read
25 ms at the 99th percentile, the machine, not the code; the frame's time
is the gate's perf run. Pin 2.3 climbs Crown Avenue (the crest's landing
comes with the crossings, slice 4); 2.4 drives 820 m of the highway.

#### Slice 3 — the ground's look and the coast (1 day)

`src/render/island/GroundView.ts`: a mesh per chunk from the height field,
4 m quads, merged where flat (the low tier's budget), vertex colours by
surface: grass, dirt, sand, rock, paving; the coast's edges (the cliffs'
faces and rocks, the quays' walls and bollards, the beach into the water);
the sea (M8.8's) round the new coast, the basin and the bay. The surface
map (M8.8 slice 9) covers the island's bounds and gains SAND (grip 0.85,
drag as dirt's; the 4×4's row 1.0). The wheels read it as today.

Pins: 3.1 a chunk's ground under 2,400 triangles; 3.2 the surface at ten
known points (the beach's sand, the golf's grass, the quarry's dirt, a road
graded later reads the paving it is laid on); 3.3 the sand's grip in the
surface table and the 4×4's row.

As built: `src/render/island/GroundView.ts` is an RTIN (`rtin.ts`) over
65 × 65 points a chunk (3.9 m), each point its own allowance (3 cm over
under a road's strip or a paved slab, 8 cm at the water, 30 cm on the open
ground, 3 m under the sea), cut by marching triangles along the steep
shores (and a road's bank over the water kept), the cut hung with the
shore's face, skirts at the chunks' borders; 366 triangles a chunk at the
median, 1,518 at most. The quads were never merged: the RTIN is coarse
where flat by itself. The colours are the corners' (the grass blended by
height), a steep face all rock; the golf's fairway waits for slice 10. The
roads' strips lie on the ground at three points across, with skirts; the
paved places are slabs over it (`IslandView`). The coast in the ground: a
beach meets the sea at its level, a steep edge keeps the land's height
2.4 m past its line (the physics' cell) with the island's wall on the land
behind it, raised with the land (at 4.3 m it stood inside the cliffs);
the beach's wall stands 6 m out, where the water is a hand deep. Added:
3.4 a car under the sea a second is put back on the road; and a braked

### Phase C — the roads

## 7. API facts and traps

- **The world's axes are +X west and +Z north**: the full map draws −x to the
  right and −z down (`bigMapProject`). The sketches draw north up and east
  right, so `plan.ts` writes the sketches' numbers and turns each point a half
  turn (`W`) into the world's; the atlas draws with the game's projection.
  (DESIGN §6's compass names for the grid's districts were written the other
  way round: on the game's map Crown Heights stands to the south-east.)
- **Rapier's height field misses a ray cast exactly straight down** (measured
  in slice 2: most such rays pass through; a lean of 1e-3 hits exactly). The
  wheels' plumb rays and the landing ray lean by `Vehicle.plumbTilt`
  (`PLUMB_TILT`, 1e-4, set on the island only, so the grid's physics is
  untouched); any other ray cast down at the island's ground leans too.
- A chunk's heights take about 20 ms to work out: they are worked out ahead
  (`Island.prefetch`, four columns a step over the 5×5 round the car) and the
  ring's height fields are built one a step; a trimesh instead cost 40–50 ms a
  chunk to build and was not taken.
- The upright chassis does not collide with the terrain group
  (`GROUPS_CHASSIS_UPRIGHT` in `src/sim/collision.ts`); the wheels' rays
  meet it. The height field is terrain.
- Rapier's height field: check `ColliderDesc.heightfield`'s signature and
  its heights' order (rows along which axis) in the installed
  `@dimforge/rapier3d-compat` types before slice 2; a transposed field is
  a mirrored island.
- The surface map (M8.8 slice 9) is sized by `CITY_HALF`; the island's
  bounds are larger (the causeway reaches x 1065).
- The sea (M8.8 slice 19) sits at `SEA.level` −0.5 with its slipways on the
  grid's seawall; the island's coast is the new boundary.
- `chunkCoord` clamps to −3..3 and `districtAt` is by quadrant on the grid;
  the island reads its own (`Island.chunkCoord`, the plan's areas).
- `LANE_INSET` (23 m) fits the grid's wide crossings; the hill's 90 m blocks
  leave 44 m of lane between two, and a roundabout's spokes need their own.
- `projectOnLane` counts a lane's height: the street under the viaduct and
  the bridge is not the deck over it; keep the lanes' `y` true.
- Fixed coordinates of the grid (the breakers, the donut shop, the stash
  spots, the slipways, the sea trial's buoys, the mega-ramp, the drop-off
  lots, the cold open's `LOOP`) stay the grid's until slices 14–15 move them
  to the plan.
- The 49 chunks' index `(cz+3)*7+(cx+3)` is baked into the coins' ids, the
  billboards' ids (`Collectibles.smashed`, 196 slots), the props' ids,
  `City.laid`, `CityView`'s tiles, `cover.node` and `ColdOpen.nodeAt`; the
  island's chunks (11 × 9 of 225 m) have their own index, and the save's hunts
  (billboards smashed, jumps found) take a migration or a reset at the switch.
- Flat ground, everywhere: the traffic's junction curves are at y = 0
  (`lanes.ts`); parked cars, stash cars, roadblocks and the donut shop's
  cruisers stand at y = 0 (`Traffic.placeAtPoint`, `puppetOff`, `takeOver`);
  props rest on y = 0; `COIN_HEIGHT` is absolute; the rings, signs, doors and
  poles draw at absolute heights (`render/run/signs.ts`, `MarkerView`,
  `HideoutView`); debris bounces at 0 and sparks die under 0.02; the surface
  map lays only statics whose top is under 0.4 m; `glow.ts` reads world y as
  height over the ground. Each reads the ground's height from slice 2 or from
  its own slice.
- Height tests that mean "not on the overpass" break on a hill: roadblocks
  only where a lane's y < 0.05 (`cover.ts`), the coins' verge hook skipped
  where y > 0.05 (`coins.ts`), a finish needs y === 0 (`place.ts`); they
  become "on the ground's height".
- The bot's `TrackSample` has no y. The chase camera's occlusion ray meets
  SOLID only: a hill or the tunnel's roof would not pull the camera in, so
  the tunnel's roof is solid and the camera's ray reads the terrain (slice 8).
- The radar and the full map assume a square island round the origin
  (`mapHalf`), quadrant tints, a highway ring at the largest |x| or |z| and
  the grid's axis lines (`minimap.ts`, `minimapModel.ts`, `bigmap.ts`).
- About 110 of the 157 test files build `map: 'city'` worlds; the layout's
  fingerprints (`look.test.ts` 11.1, `street.long.test.ts` 0.2) and the baked
  jobs (`jobs.test.ts` 1.1) change once, at the switch.
