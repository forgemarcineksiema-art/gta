Read, in this order, before touching anything: `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/BRIEF.md`

## 0. How to work on this milestone

- **Language, autonomy, scope, honesty**: as in `docs/M5_PLAN.md` §0.
- **Pace** (`CLAUDE.md`): a slice is the code, its pins, the quick verify,
  one commit, eight lines in PROGRESS. No e2e, no screens, no perf, no
  browser checks per slice; all of it at the gate.
- **Marcin's notes come first**, each a slice ahead of the queue.
- **Subtract, never add.** A slice that leaves the driving screen with more
  than it found is wrong. No new system, no new number on screen, no new
  art but the sack glyph.
- **The pure part is the pinned part.** Each slice puts its decision in a
  small pure module under `src/ui/` (what shows, what speaks, which page)
  and pins that in `tests/ui/`, as `lanes.ts` and `wallGrid.ts` are. The
  DOM follows the module and is looked at once, at the gate.
- **Plain words in every message to him**: the bank, the bag, the stars,
  the wall's pages; not masks, arbiters or corners-as-code.

## 1. Scope

### 1.1 In scope (§3 slices 0–5)

1. One bank: a road coin lands in the bank; one number, one word (§17.4).
2. The four corners: seven things on a calm drive, the rest on its moment
   (§17.2).
3. One voice: one message at the top, two pops at the side, one text per
   event (§17.3).
4. The wall in four pages (§17.5).
5. The words: §17.4's table on every screen, pinned.
6. The gate with the twelve-year-old's pass (§17.7).

### 1.2 Out of scope

Removing a system; any balance number; new jobs, props or cars; the cold
open's route and captions (its HUD is the corners like any drive); the full
map's layout (it gains the hunts' counts in a corner, nothing more);
localisation; touch and the platform (M9).

## 2. Decisions (each with its reason)

D1. **A road coin goes into the bank.** `Run.coins` and `Run.funds` go;
the `coin` event adds to `bank` (a spilled coin still to the bag);
`counts.coins` still counts. Save v6 folds a stored `coins` into `bank`.
Reason: the pool was the player's and spent as one with the bank already;
two numbers for one purse is the review's BANK-and-CASH screen. D14's
reason (a child's money is never at risk) holds: the bank is never at risk.

D2. **The bag shows from its first money, its × from ×1.3.** Reason: a
number that cannot change a decision is noise, and its appearance is the
lesson (the × arrives with the second star's pursuit, §2.6's table). The
bag's box keeps its place empty so the bank never moves.

D3. **The hunts' counters leave the driving screen.** Reason: a collection's
progress changes nothing inside a drive; the find's pop carries the count at
the one moment it matters, and GOALS and the full map keep it.

D4. **The gear, ONCOMING, the drift readout and the job line's coin count
go.** Reason: the box is automatic; the combo's word names the oncoming run
and the drift; the coins are on the road. The debug block keeps the gear.

D5. **The radar's names show four seconds on a new district and at the
drive-out.** Reason: GTA's rule; the radar answers "where", the name is
news.

D6. **The top centre carries only lines that change what you do** (§17.3's
list); the evening news, UNIT DOWN, a rival driving by and the damage news
go; nothing speaks over the busted card. Reason: the player reads slowly and
often not in English; the world tells those jokes.

D7. **A trick the combo counts never pops; two pops at most.** Reason: one
event, one text; the review counted four NEAR MISS pops beside the combo
that already said NEAR MISS.

D8. **The wall is TOTALS · CARS · STYLE · GOALS.** TUNE's rows go under the
car on CARS, PREP onto TOTALS as the next run's row, BOARD and DAILIES into
GOALS. One rewarded offer at a time. Reason: seven tabs is a menu; four is
a garage (the result, the cars, the looks, what to do next), and the booster
sits where the next run starts.

D9. **The developer's panel only with `?dev=1`**; the key hints never name
it. Reason: a children's build on a portal does not ship a tuning panel on
a key.

D10. **One name for each thing, pinned.** `tests/ui/words.test.ts` fails on
§17.4's never-words in any on-screen string (upper-case literals in
`src/ui/` and the sim's text tables). Reason: the review found four names
for money after three milestones each added one; a pin stops the fifth.

## 6. Five-minute playtest script (for the gate report)

M8's script (`docs/M8_PLAN.md` §9) first, then:

1. `?fresh=1`: the van, the first garage. How many things on the wall's
   first page, and is it plain what to press?
2. Drive out. What is on the screen with nothing happening? Count them.
3. Pick up coins: the bank ticks. Take a job: the bag appears. Is it plain
   which money is safe and which is not?
4. Two stars and a chase: the × appears beside the bag. Does it say "the
   cops make my bag worth more"?
5. A string of near misses: the combo, and no pops beside it.
6. A billboard: its pop with the count. Where are the three hunts now
   (the wall's GOALS, the map)?
7. Busted once: the card alone on the screen.
8. The wall: four pages. Buy an upgrade from CARS, a booster on TOTALS.
9. Anything a twelve-year-old would ask "what is that?" about?
