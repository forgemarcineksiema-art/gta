## 0. How to work on this milestone

- **Language, autonomy, scope, honesty**: as in `docs/M5_PLAN.md` §0.
- **Pace** (`CLAUDE.md`): a slice is the code, its pins, the quick verify,
  one commit, eight lines in PROGRESS. The long pins, the suites and the
  perf at the gate; slice 5's measurement is the one Marcin asked for.
- **Marcin's notes come first**, each a slice ahead of the queue.
- **No pop.** Nothing a player can see is moved or turned in one step: a
  pose changes by the physics, a blend, or out of view.
- **The chase read is the measure.** `tests/sim/solid.long.test.ts` drives
  the bot under a level-5 chase for 120 s and reads every lent body at
  every step (§18.1's numbers); each slice adds its line to it.

## 2. Decisions (each with its reason)

D1. **A driving lent body turns about the vertical only.** `lend()` and the
reattach lock its roll and pitch (`setEnabledRotations(false, true, false)`)
with its height; `loosen()` frees all six. Reason: the height lock without
ground contact made roll and pitch free spins with nothing to right them
(§18.1); a car driving on its lane has no business leaning.

D2. **A shaken car reattaches on its wheels, still.** Level within
`reattachLevel` (10°), under `reattachSpeed` (1 m/s) and `reattachSpin`
(0.6 rad/s) on every axis, its body within 0.3 m of its road, within
`reattachDistance` of its path. Leaning more, at rest, it is rocked back by
a capped torque about its own long axis (a kerb, a bumper); past
`disturbedMax` it is a wreck, as now. Reason: the old rule stood a car up
from 45° in one step; the new one only removes what the eye cannot see.

D3. **A wreck at rest on a side or an end is tipped over.** After
`tipAfter` (0.5 s) at rest with `|up| < 0.7` (a side) or the nose's height
over 0.7 (an end), its body gets the spin that lays it on the nearer of its
wheels or its roof; again after each `tipAfter` until it lies. Reason: a box
rests on any face; a car does not stand on its nose. A convex hull shaped
like a car was the alternative; it keeps the flat side and costs the
solver more.

D4. **A body-less wreck, abandoned or parked car keeps its full pose.** The
record holds its rotation and height (`qx..qw`, `y`), written from its body
every step it has one, drawn from the record when it has none, restored on
`lend()`; `releaseBody()` moves it onto no lane, only reads its lane
position for the lane lists. Reason: §18.1's jumping wrecks.

D5. **The four places are places a car fits.** A place is open when a unit's
box fits there (a shape test against the fixed world and the lent bodies,
units excluded); four diagonal places stand behind the four when one is
shut. Reason: a place inside a wreck made a unit push the wreck forever.

D6. **A unit held up holds.** Driving to its place, a unit moving at under a
third of its command for `holdAfter` (0.6 s) holds where it is (command 0)
for `holdFor` (1.5 s), then the places are dealt again; a held unit within
the busted range counts. Its straight line goes round the nearest lent body
on it, as it goes round the player's car now. Reason: a velocity command
into a car is a shove, and the shove built the pile.

D7. **From the bust to the card's close every unit stands still**; after it
each drives off along its lane; one that has not moved 2 m in 3 s and is
out of view is released. Reason: the card is the end of the chase; a shove
under it moved the player's car out from under the officer.

D8. **The officer never stands in a car.** He comes out of the unit's side
away from other cars, walks to the window by a corner of the player's car
when the straight line crosses it, is held out of every car's footprint
(grown by his 0.35 m) by a slide along its side, and writes at the
driver's window or, when a car stands within 1 m of it, the passenger's;
the window follows the car. Reason: screenshot 4.

D9. **A car in the camera's way is drawn through a screen door.** A
per-instance fade on the traffic's and the police's instanced meshes; the
fragment discards by a 4×4 ordered pattern (no transparency, no sorting, no
extra draw call); a car whose box meets the segment from the camera to the
player's car, grown by 0.4 m, or within 2.5 m of the camera, fades to a
quarter in 0.15 s and back. Reason: screenshots 1, 3 and 5; a fade keeps
the camera where the player put it.

D10. **The frames are measured before anything is changed for them.** One
headed run on Marcin's laptop at his window size in each scene, the frame
split into the sim, the drawing's JavaScript and the GPU wait, against
0.8.5 in alternation (`perf-noise-ab-method`); the largest cost is fixed
and measured again. The city's loading is spread whatever the run says: a
part's colliders are added over steps under a budget, nearest first, from
further out, so no step spends over 8 ms on it. Reason: the sim is 0.8 ms
a step in the pile (§18.1); guessing at the drawing wastes his evening.
