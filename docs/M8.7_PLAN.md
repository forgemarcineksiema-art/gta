# M8.7 "The way" — implementation plan

Executor: the agent that starts on Marcin's word. Reviewer: Claude, at the
gate. Director and the only tester: Marcin. This document is the milestone
contract for the player's way (`docs/DESIGN.md` §20): one answer to each
question a player has (what now: the goal line; where: the route on the
radar; what is it: the sign over the ring), no arrow, a goal that holds, a
job taken by choice, nothing opened in a chase, the kinds one at a time.
Written 2026-09-24 against commit `6547595`. M8.6's gate (first pass at
`3a1a3f7`: jobs 1.7 and botPolicy 5.8 red; the suites, the balance and the
perf not run) closes inside this gate, as M8's closed inside M8.5's. The
platform keeps its number and its contract, `docs/M9_PLAN.md`, and starts
after this gate.

Read, in this order, before touching anything: `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/DESIGN.md`
§20 in full (then §13.4 for the goal line, §17 for the screen, §19 for the
words), `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` (the road graph; records 97 and 98 for the
folders), `docs/PROGRESS.md` (newest first), then this file. Run
`npm run verify`; green before the first edit.

## 0. How to work on this milestone

- **Language, autonomy, scope, honesty**: as in `docs/M5_PLAN.md` §0.
- **Pace** (`CLAUDE.md`): a slice is the code, its pins, the quick verify,
  one commit, eight lines in PROGRESS. The long pins, the suites, the perf
  and the reading checks (§20.5) at the gate.
- **Marcin's notes come first**, each a slice ahead of the queue.
- **Every slice leaves the game playable and better**: he plays each build.
  The arrow goes in the slice that draws the route (slice 1), not before.
- **One answer per question** is the review's rule: a change that gives a
  second answer to what now, where or what is it goes back (§20.4 lists the
  ones already turned down).
- **Every new word has its Polish in the same slice** (`src/ui/pl.ts`,
  `tests/ui/lang.test.ts`) and its place in `tests/ui/words.test.ts`.

## 2. Decisions (each with its reason)

D1. **The goal holds** (`src/sim/run/goal.ts`). The precedence stays (a
running job, the pursuit, the chain's step, BANK IT, the board, TAKE A
JOB). Where a goal kind picks a ring, the pick holds: re-read every
`repick` (0.5 s), the kept ring is dropped only when it is taken or closed,
or when another shown, open ring's road distance is under `switchShare`
(0.6) of the kept one's and at least `switchGain` (150 m) less. A pick from
the full map (D7) holds until it is taken or closed. Road distances come
from D2's forward pass. Reason: the nearest ring by straight line, taken
every frame, swings the goal between two rings and prefers a ring behind a
block (§20.1).

D2. **The way** (new `src/sim/run/way.ts`, `sim.way`). Each lane's incoming
lanes are listed once when the graph is built (`src/sim/city/roads.ts`).
Two passes over the directed lanes (U-turn links included), the plain
O(n²) Dijkstra of `city/route.ts` rewritten into preallocated typed arrays
(a few hundred lanes; no heap needed):

- a **reverse pass** from the goal's lane gives every lane its distance to
  the goal and its next lane (the field); it runs when the goal changes,
  when the goal's point moves over 10 m (a moving goal, the wanted car or a
  hunted rival, is read every `movingReplan`, 1 s), and when a roadblock
  goes up or down;
- a **forward pass** from the car's lane gives every ring its road distance
  for D1, every `repick`.

The car's lane is the nearest lane whose direction is within 90° of the
car's heading (else the nearest), found with `alongLane`; a car off the
road takes the nearest lane's point as the route's first point. The route
is up to `routePoints` (96) world points, the car, then the lanes' points
along the next lanes, then the goal's point; rebuilt every 0.25 s into a
preallocated array; its length is the line's distance. A lane through an
active roadblock site costs `roadblockCost` (800 m) more. No allocation per
step (the gc long pin reads the way). Reason: a field makes a missed turn a
lookup, not a search, and the whole city costs under a millisecond.

D3. **The radar draws the route** (`src/ui/map/minimap.ts`,
`src/ui/map/minimapModel.ts`). Over the roads, under the units and the
car: a `routeWidth` (5 px) line in cyan (`#2bd1ff`) on a dark edge 4 px
wider, round joins; the goal's badge (D5's outline, 16 px) at its end,
clamped to the rim by the landmarks' rule when outside. The draw-in: when
the goal changes or the route's first lane changes, the line is drawn from
the car outward over `drawIn` (0.5 s); otherwise it is still. The radar's
repaint keeps its cadence; the route's points go through `project`. The
full map (`src/ui/map/bigmap.ts`) draws the whole route at 4 px. Reason:
the radar is the one answer to "where" (§17.2); a still line that moves
only when it matters pulls the eye at the right moment.

D4. **The arrow goes** (`src/render/run/Arrow.ts` deleted, its wiring in
`src/render/Renderer.ts`). `Jobs.arrowTarget` and `Jobs.idleTarget` give
way to `sim.way` (the goal's point, a running job's or not); the pins that
read them move to the way (jobs 1.3, the chain's long pin), and corners
1.1 counts six. The cards' FOLLOW THE ARROW becomes FOLLOW THE LINE (JEDŹ
ZA LINIĄ). Reason: Marcin's decision (§20.3 rule 1).

D5. **The sign** (`src/render/run/MarkerView.ts`; the outlines in new
`src/sim/glyphs.ts`, pure data that the render and the ui both read). Per
kind one closed outline in a unit square: a parcel (delivery), a car key
(steal to order), a police light (escape), a stopwatch (time trial), a
chequered flag (street race), a crash star (takedown rage), a hammer
(mayhem), a taxi sign (a fare), the house (the garage); a rival's sign
carries its poster number, drawn from digit outlines in the same file (no
texture). The sign: the pole (steel, 3 m), a
disc 1.4 m across (24 segments) with a dark rim 0.08 m wide, the outline
extruded 2 cm in the panel's dark; unlit like the markers; turned about the
vertical to the camera every frame; instanced by part and by kind (at most
ten draw calls for every sign, under 400 triangles a sign). The goal's
sign: its rim cyan, a bob of ±0.12 m at 1 Hz, its ring pulsing as today; a
closed sign: face and outline grey; the others white and still. The pay:
one world-anchored DOM label for the nearest open sign within `payRange`
(100 m) and in front of the camera, in the line's yellow type. **The goal
wears its sign**: a ring's; the garage door's house under BANK IT and BUY;
the key 2.2 m over the wanted car's roof while hunting, within today's
`ringRange` and in front of the camera, instead of the ring under it; a
rival's number at the kerb. A fare's hailer wears the taxi sign instead of
the yellow beacon. The ring on the ground: white, cyan for the goal, grey
closed; lit cyan for 0.4 s at a start. Reason: §20.3 rules 5–6; one drawing
at four sizes keeps the world, the radar, the map and the line saying the
same thing.

D6. **Colour by state** (`src/ui/styles.css`, `src/render/run/MarkerView.ts`,
`src/ui/map/*`, `src/ui/hud/jobs.ts`, `src/ui/hud/hud.ts`). `KIND_COLORS`
and `JOB_COLORS` go; three state colours: open ink `#f7f3ea`, goal cyan
`#2bd1ff`, closed grey `#8d8a96`. The line's kind words in ink; the job
card's edge cyan; a zone's edge (rage, mayhem) cyan on the road and on the
radar; the boost bar's fill ink; the rival's ring white; the swap prompt
keeps cyan (a swap is a move, and in a steal-to-order the goal). The full
map's key shows the outlines. Reason: §20.3 rule 6: one colour, one
meaning; the colour-blind read the shape.

D7. **The line names the goal; the full map picks it** (`src/ui/hud/jobs.ts`,
`src/ui/map/bigmap.ts`, `sim.way`). The line: the badge (the kind's outline
as inline SVG, 30 px, in a cyan rim when it is the goal), the name
(`KIND_TITLE`), the pay in yellow, the distance by road:
`[parcel] DELIVERY +1,200 · 450 M`. BANK IT and BUY carry the house; LOSE
THEM carries none (danger red as today); the chain's steps keep their words
with their target's badge. The full map: a click within 14 px of a sign's
glyph makes it the goal (`way.pick(id)`); a click on the car or on nothing
clears the pick. Reason: §20.3 rules 4 and 7.

D8. **A job is taken by rolling in** (`src/sim/jobs/Jobs.ts`,
`src/sim/balance.ts`). A ring starts its job when the car is inside
`markerRadius` under `startSpeed` (5.5 m/s, 20 km/h); the rearm rule stays;
a crossing at `startSpeed` or over is a fast pass. A start pushes
`jobTaken` (a new event): the ring's light and a short chime
(`src/audio/Sfx.ts`). SLOW DOWN IN THE RING (ZWOLNIJ W KÓŁKU) goes to the
one message at the top (`src/ui/hud/voice.ts`) on the first ring while the
chain's first step is open, and on the first three fast passes of a
session. A rival keeps its pull-up (7 m, 4 m/s); a fare's hailer keeps
"stop alongside". Reason: §20.3 rule 8; a ring crossed at speed on the way
to the garage no longer takes the player's goal away.

D9. **Nothing opens in a chase** (`src/sim/jobs/Jobs.ts`,
`src/sim/jobs/Fares.ts`, `src/sim/board/Board.ts`). `Jobs.open(d)` (was
`live`) is false for every def while no job runs and `pursuit.state` is not
`idle`, the cold open's own def excepted; `Jobs.shown(d)` (D10) keeps the
views drawing it, grey. No fare hails in a chase and a hailer drops; a
rival waits. Reason: §20.3 rule 9, GTA V's rule; stars alone close
nothing, since they multiply the bag.

D10. **The kinds one at a time** (`src/sim/jobs/Jobs.ts`,
`src/sim/run/goal.ts`, `src/sim/balance.ts`; the card in
`src/ui/hud/jobs.ts`; the rise in `src/render/run/MarkerView.ts`).
`BALANCE.reveal` maps each placed kind to the chain bit that shows it:
delivery none; race and trial `take`; rage and mayhem `bank`; escape `car`;
order `escape` (the step that asks for one follows it). A kind is shown when
its bit is set; `open` and `goalFor` see shown kinds only. When a bit
reveals kinds, after the step's own card, one card per kind:
`NEW: {kind}` (NOWOŚĆ: …) with its outline, and its signs rise from the
ground over 0.6 s. The duel's gate (the board) and the fare's (the taxi)
stand. `?reveal=all` shows every kind (a QA hook, `docs/DEV.md`). No save
field: the chain decides. Reason: §20.3 rule 10; §3.2's "every job open
from the first run" put 28 rings of seven kinds in front of a child who
knew none.

D11. **The cold open teaches it** (`src/sim/run/ColdOpen.ts`,
`src/ui/hud/coldOpen.ts`). The route on the radar to its ring from the first
second, then to the garage; its ring takes D8's rule with the caption SLOW
DOWN IN THE RING; it stays open under its own pursuit (D9's exception). Its
coin line, captions and timing otherwise stand. Reason: §20.3 rule 11; the
rule is learnt in the first minute.

D12. **The bots and the long pins.** Every bot that starts a job by driving
in brakes under `startSpeed` in a ring it means to take (`src/app/jobBot.ts`,
`src/app/botPolicy.ts`, the chain's and the jobs' long pins); the order
hunter may read the way's field for the wanted car (it would close the
naive hunter's line in `docs/BACKLOG.md`). Jobs 1.7 is the route's promise,
every delivery made inside its limit on the lane path: it is green at the
gate by its cause (M8.6 left 5 of 18 deliveries slower than 0.8.5 with no
cause found) and never by loosening it. No bisect of chaotic runs
(`CLAUDE.md`, pace): read the failing delivery, fix what makes it slow.
Reason: this milestone tells a child to follow the route, so the route has
to be one a car can make.
