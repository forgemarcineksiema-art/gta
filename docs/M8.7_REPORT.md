two wheels, a truck on a car, the officer inside his car, frames dropping):

- **The officer walks round** (slice 3): out of the clear door, to the
  driver's window unless a car stands at it, round the car by its corners;

### M8.7, the way (DESIGN §20)

Marcin: the arrow was unintuitive, the radar's dots said nothing, a job was
"only some stick", and in a chase nobody knew whether to flee or take a job.
Decided in the talk: one answer to each question, and no arrow.

- **The goal holds** (slice 0): `sim/run/way.ts` keeps the goal the line
  names and its route. Road distances, not straight lines; another ring
  takes over only when under 0.6 of the held one's distance and 150 m
  nearer. Forty route-following drives changed the goal once and never back
  (the straight-line rule: 25 times).
- **The route on the radar, no arrow** (slice 1): a 5 px cyan line on a dark
  edge from the car to the goal's badge, drawn out over 0.5 s when the goal
  or the route changes, still otherwise; the whole route on the full map;
  the arrow gone; the cards say FOLLOW THE LINE.
- **Taken by choice, not in a chase** (slice 2): a ring starts its job when
  rolled into under 20 km/h; driven through faster it says SLOW DOWN IN THE
  RING (the first job to come, then the session's first three passes); with
  the police on the player nothing opens (grey), the cold open's ring
  excepted.
- **The signs and the colours** (slice 3): one pictogram per kind (parcel,
  key, police light, stopwatch, chequered flag, crash star, claw hammer,
  taxi, house, star, a rival's number) on a round sign on a pole in the
  world, on the radar, the full map and the line; open white, the goal cyan,
  closed grey; the nearest open sign ahead within 100 m shows its pay. The
  kinds' colours are gone.
- **One kind at a time, the pick, the detour** (slice 4): deliveries from
  the start; races and trials after the first job; rage and mayhem after the
  first bank; escapes after the first car; orders after the escape at two
  stars, each with a NEW card and its signs rising. A click on a sign on the
  full map pins it as the goal. A roadblock up costs its lane 800 m, so the
  route goes round it.
- **At the gate**: M8.6's two red pins by their causes. Jobs 1.7: two
  traffic cars nose to nose in a junction's box (a forced entry and a
  turner) waited for each other for ever, and the queue behind them held
  delivery #12 past its clock; now the one further past its line steers out
  beside the other and crawls past (pin M8.7 5.1; #12 in 57 s of 62).
  Botpolicy 5.8: the bot shoved a wreck onto a queued car at 0.6 m/s till it
  reset (since M8.6 a wreck no longer slides off a push); the policy bots go
  round a block, as the cold open's bot does. The radar's signs were 13.4 px
  across, under §20.5's 14 at 800×450: now 14.5. The gc pin counted the
  bot's reset teleport as streaming; it skips that step. `verify:gate` runs
  a worker a core (seven on four cores doubled the timed pins).

## How to run

- `npm start`, then `http://localhost:4173/`; the stamp `0.8.7+<commit>` on
  the pause screen. `?fresh=1` for a first run (the cold open, then the
  deliveries; the other kinds come out with the chain); `?reveal=all` shows
  every kind at once, and
  `?job=<kind>` implies it. `?dev=1` for the tuning panel.
- Hold Tab for the full map; a click on a sign there makes it the goal, a
  click on the car or on nothing lets it go.
- `npm run verify`, `verify:gate`, `game`, `heat`, `city`, `life`,
  `screens`, `boot`, `perf`, `balance` (`docs/DEV.md`).

## Five-minute playtest script

1. `?fresh=1`: the cold open. Is the cyan line on the radar there from the
   first second, and does the van get to the parcel by it alone?
2. At the delivery ring: roll in slowly. Drive through one fast: SLOW DOWN
   IN THE RING at the top, nothing starts.
3. Out of the garage: what is on the screen with nothing happening? Six
   things (the line, the stars, the bank, the radar, the speed, the boost).
4. Follow the line to a job without looking at anything else. Does the goal
   hold while you drive past other rings?
5. Miss a turn on purpose: the line redraws from the car in half a second.
6. Get the police on you: the rings go grey and LOSE THEM takes the line.
7. After the first job: the NEW card for races and trials, their signs rise.
8. Hold Tab, click a far sign: the line and the route take it.
9. In a five-star chase (M8.6): cars and wrecks on their wheels or roofs,
   never on a nose; the officer at your window, never inside a car; your car
   always seen.

  pass had not run them either. M8.6's frames were measured in its slice 5 on
  Marcin's laptop; M8.6 and M8.7 together have no perf reading yet. The next

  suite, 64 s alone. Other sessions' browser tests on the same machine made
  it worse (one run's lint took 356 s against 55). Not loosened.
- **The quick verify's tests at 91–105 s** against the rule's 60 (91 before
  M8.7; M8.6 grew them). BACKLOG.
- **A route from a car facing away from its goal** runs on to the next
  junction and back (a V on the radar at a job's start): the way leaves from
  the car's lane in its heading (D2); turning round redraws it in half a
  second. To watch in play.
- **The first-five-minutes walk** was a bot: it took the first job and banked
  it, then stood at the door while the line said BUY THE COMPACT, so the
  second job is Marcin's reading.
- **The nose-to-nose go-round** steers up to 3.5 m off its path in a
  junction's box; watch a busy crossing.
- **The heap** (M8.5: 58–65 MB with traffic) was not measured again.
