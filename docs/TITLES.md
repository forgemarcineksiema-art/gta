# Working titles

Five proposals (brief §2). All original; none reference existing games, brands or trademarks as far as a search of app stores and Steam on 2026-09-20 shows. Marcin picks one; until then the build says "Untitled Driving Game".

| # | Title | Why it fits | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Swerve City** | Says "driving" and "city" in two words, the verb is the core skill (near misses, drifts, dodging pedestrians), easy to say and search. | Recommended. Short enough for a thumbnail. |
| 2 | **Heatline** | The wanted level ("heat") is the spine of the loop; "line" hints at racing lines and the highway. | More serious tone than the game; works if the police fantasy leads. |
| 3 | **Joyride Junction** | Alliterative, cheerful, tells you it's about stealing cars for fun without the word "theft". | Longer; "junction" reads a little young. |
| 4 | **Full Send City** | Meme-adjacent slang for going all out; matches boost/takedown energy and the CrazyGames audience. | Slang may date; check locale readability. |
| 5 | **Sirens & Swerves** | Police chases plus dodging, playful rhythm. | Ampersand is awkward in URLs and store fields; would ship as "Sirens and Swerves". |

Rejected on the way: anything with "Grand", "Theft", "Auto", "Need", "Speed", "Burnout", "Saints" (references), "Chromebook-safe" length over 20 characters, and titles already used by published games ("Joyride", "Getaway", "Wreckfest"-style compounds).
