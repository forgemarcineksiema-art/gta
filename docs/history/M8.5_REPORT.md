Date: 2026-09-24. Contracts: `docs/history/M8_PLAN.md` (the chaos, slices 0–9,

## What was built

### M8, the chaos (DESIGN §16)

- **The street is made of things with mass** (slices 0–2): lamp posts,
  street trees, benches, bins, railings, hydrants and the rest are props
  with a mass, a base's strength and a material. The car's contact with
  them is decided by a closed-form rule before the physics step: a post
  that holds is a wall, one that goes folds away from the car. A pool of
  16 bodies; props lie where they fall and heal out of sight; the thick
  trees' trunks are walls, the street saplings snap.
- **What stands where** (slices 3, 4, 8): each district's set (Crown's
  meters, newsstands and café terraces, the Works' cones and barrels, the
  Gardens' fences, flamingos and fruit stands, the Quay's deckchairs and
  fish stalls), the mayhem zones' markets, the cold open's terrace.
- **The spectacle** (slice 5): debris by material, sparks off metal, the
  hydrant's water, a voice per material.
- **The pay** (slice 6): a smash is a combo trick (the thing's name its
  word), fills the boost and is a crime; CITY DAMAGE on the wall.
- **Everyone else** (slice 7): the police and a shoved car knock by the
  same rule; traffic ploughs lying props aside; walkers dodge flying ones.
- **The long game** (slice 9): save v5, two dailies, Big Bernie's SMASH
  300 THINGS.

### M8.5, the clean screen (DESIGN §17)

Marcin's review: too dense for a child. What changed, in the order a
twelve-year-old meets it:

- **One bank** (slice 0): a road coin lands in the bank the moment it is
  picked; the wall's BANK and the garage's footer are one number (CASH is
  gone). Save v6 folds an old coin pool into the bank.
- **The four corners** (slice 1): a calm drive shows seven things (the goal
  line and the arrow, the stars and the bank, the radar, the speed and the
  boost), fourteen before. The bag shows with its first money and its ×
  from ×1.3; the district's name for four seconds; the damage once dented;
  the combo while it runs. The gear, ONCOMING, the drift readout, the three
  hunt counters (now a find's pop, the map's head and GOALS) and the job
  line's coin count are gone. Behind a door and on the busted card the
  driving screen goes whole. The tuning panel and its key hint only with
  `?dev=1`.
- **One voice** (slice 2): every event speaks in one place with one text;
  the top only for what changes what you do; two pops at most; the combo's
  tricks never pop; the evening news, UNIT DOWN, a rival driving by and the
  damage news are gone; nothing speaks over the wall or the card.
- **The wall in four** (slice 3): TOTALS · CARS · STYLE · GOALS instead of
  seven tabs. TOTALS says BANKED (NEW BEST when it is), one line of sums,
  the next goal and the counts. CARS holds the chosen car's upgrades and the
  next run's boosters under the cars (not TOTALS as §17.5 first said: W
  there drives out, and a row of buttons would be one key from spending by
  mistake). GOALS holds the next goal, the day's three and the streak, the
  board and the hunts.
- **The words** (slice 4): one name for each thing: BANK and BAG, the stars
  (never HEAT or LEVEL), GARAGE, COPS, COMBO, BAG BONUS (the fence), the
  arrow for a job's end; a pin reads every on-screen string.
- **At the gate**: the job and step cards' stray E keycap (on every card
  since M5.5: the keycap's style outranked the card's), the trials' row of
  dashes on GOALS (medal counts now, once there is one), the streak on
  TODAY's line; and the boot's city build made cheaper (below).

## Tuning knobs and where they live

- `src/ui/corners.ts`: what the driving screen shows and when
  (`PLACE_SECONDS` 4, the bag's and the ×'s thresholds in `drive`).
- `src/ui/voice.ts`: where each event speaks (`WHERE`), its words, the
  stars' news (`STARS_NEWS`), the pops (`POP_SLOTS` 2, `POP_SECONDS` 1.2);
  the radio's pause (`DISPATCH_EVERY` in `hud.ts`).
- `src/ui/wallPages.ts`: the wall's pages and the page of every button;
  `src/ui/totals.ts`: TOTALS' and the busted card's words.
- `tests/ui/words.test.ts`: the retired words (`NEVER`), each with the one
  to say instead.
- `src/sim/balance.ts` (`PROPS`) and `src/sim/city/props.ts` (`PROP_TYPES`,
  `PROP_LINES`, `PROP_KEEP`, `MARKET`): the chaos's numbers, as M8 set them.

## How to run

- `npm start`, then `http://localhost:4173/`; the stamp `0.8.5+<commit>` on
  the pause screen. `?dev=1` for the tuning panel (the backtick) and the
  debug block.
- The wall: A/D the tabs, W into a page (CARS: the cars' row, then a row an
  upgrade, a row a booster; Space or Enter takes), S back; W on TOTALS
  drives out.
- `npm run verify`, `verify:gate`, `game`, `heat`, `city`, `life`,
  `screens`, `boot`, `perf`, `balance` (`docs/DEV.md`).

## Five-minute playtest script

M8's first (`docs/history/M8_PLAN.md` §9), then the screen:

1. `?fresh=1`: the van, the first garage. Is it plain what to press?
2. Drive out. What is on the screen with nothing happening? Count it.
3. Pick up coins: the bank ticks. Take a job: the bag appears. Is it plain
   which money is safe and which is not?
4. Two stars and a chase: the × appears beside the bag.
5. A string of near misses: the combo top left, and no pops beside it.
6. A billboard: its pop with the count. The three hunts on the map (hold
   Tab) and on GOALS.
7. Busted once: the card alone on the screen.
8. The wall: four pages. An upgrade and a booster from CARS.
9. Anything a twelve-year-old would ask "what is that?" about.

## Known issues

- **The JS heap: 58–65 MB with traffic against M8's own target of 50** (0.7.0:
  48; without traffic both 45–61, as the collector falls). The brief's 250 MB
  holds with room. M8's props hold it (their arrays, the 96 debris, the
  prop meshes merged into the chunks' geometry); where, at the next perf
  pass. BACKLOG.
- **The quick verify's tests at 60–63 s** against the rule's 60 (M8 grew
  them; slice 6 said the gate would trim them, and it did not). BACKLOG:
  the slowest pins into the long set.
- **The life suite's frame p95 sits on its quantum**: frame times fall on
  whole vsyncs, and a run with 5 % of its frames at two lands on exactly
  33.4 ms against the `< 33.4` (red twice here, twice at M7). The limit is
  the brief's; not loosened.
- **A single time to control can pass 5 s in a slow machine state** (5.67 s
  in the city suite before the speedups, when the A/B's medians were 4.3 and
  4.7); the medians now 3.9 and 4.0.
- **The boosters are on CARS, not TOTALS** as §17.5 first said (slice 3,
  the reason in D8). If a player never opens CARS, the lawyer and the bag
  bonus go unseen; the rewarded offer the door makes is TOTALS' own.
